- 👋 Hi, I’m @100000s
- 👀 I’m developing an app and ecosystem for the circulation of pre-loaded satoshi-denominated banknotes in four denominations, with oracles signalling predetermined events - including a timed and escrowed sponsorship auction mechanism triggered, in part, by fluctuations in the valuation of the BTC/USD trading pair.
- 🌱 I’m also exploring the options available for marketing this product for common use with initial focus on the cash-first LATAM region, and other bitcoin-friendly countries with limited internet access.
- 💞️ I’m looking to collaborate with coders familiar with the pertenant technical requirements for this project, including the integration of Solidity smart contracts into the Bitcoin timechain via RSK. You can view the overall plan here: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1OaWekWYB5rH53H3UFq9-K0XVbyYSJdOawh3lsQGoS0I/edit?usp=drivesdk.
